House extension questions

Do I need planning permission for a house extension in Ireland?

A rear extension does not need planning permission if it adds no more than 45 square metres to the original floor area of the house, is no higher than the house and leaves at least 25 square metres of open space at the back. The 45 square metres includes any earlier extensions; the limit rose from 40 square metres on 27 July 2026. Floor space above ground level is limited to 12 square metres for a terraced or semi-detached house and 20 square metres for a detached house, and must be at least 2 metres from any boundary. An extension beyond these limits needs planning permission.

How long does a house extension take?

Our kitchen extension in Marino and our rear extension in Phibsboro each took two months from demolition to finishes. Larger projects that combine an extension with a renovation, such as Glenageary, took eight months.

How much does a house extension cost?

The price depends on the size of the extension, the structural work, the glazing and the level of finish. We visit your home to see the space and talk through what you want, then send a detailed price and plan for the work. Call 089 276 1391 or use the contact form to arrange a visit.

Can you work with my architect or engineer?

Yes. We build to your engineer's specification and work with them through the project, so the structure is inspected and signed off.
