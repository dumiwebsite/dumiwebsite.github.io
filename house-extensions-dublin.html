Extensions built from start to finish

An extension changes how the whole house works, so it pays to have one team take it from the first visit to the final finishes. Delta Construct builds extensions across Dublin and Wicklow: the demolition and foundations, the walls, steel and roof, the drainage and plumbing, and the finishes that make the new room feel part of the house.

Recent work includes a kitchen and dining extension in Marino with three skylights, a rear extension in Phibsborough, a glass extension in Wicklow that looks out over the fields, and an extension built over a garage.

What we build

  • Rear and side extensions
  • Kitchen and dining extensions with skylights and sliding doors
  • Glass extensions and apex roofs
  • Extensions over a garage
  • Extensions with a bathroom or utility room

What the work covers

  • Demolition and removal of the old rear structure
  • Excavation and concrete foundations
  • Insulated floors with a radon barrier
  • Drainage, and moving the kitchen and other plumbing
  • Walls, steel lintels and joining the extension to the house
  • Roof structure and weatherproof membrane
  • Insulation and plasterboard
  • Skylights, doors and windows
  • Wall, floor and ceiling finishes
