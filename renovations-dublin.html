Renovations, start to finish

A good renovation makes an existing house work the way you live now. Delta Construct renovates homes across Dublin: opening up rooms, putting in steel beams, repairing structure, and finishing kitchens, floors and living spaces to a high standard.

Our renovation in Glenageary was a complete house makeover over eight months, with a new steel structure, an extension and new finishes throughout. We take on smaller jobs too: knocking through walls, an upstairs revamp, or a wall and fireplace taken out to make way for a wide folding door.

Questions

Can you take down a wall between two rooms?

Yes. We open up rooms regularly, including putting in steel beams, built to your engineer's specification and signed off by them.

Do you work on older houses?

Yes. Recent work includes structural repairs to a 1908 red-brick terrace in Phibsborough, where failing timber lintels and settlement along the back wall had to be put right.
