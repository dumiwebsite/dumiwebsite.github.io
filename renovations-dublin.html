Renovation contractors for Dublin homes

Delta Construct renovates houses across Dublin, from a single kitchen or bathroom to the whole home. The work ranges from taking out walls and fitting steel beams to rewiring, insulating, plastering and finishing kitchens, bathrooms and floors.

We plan the job with you and price it in detail, and our reviews describe projects finished on time and on budget. Recent renovations include Glenageary, Clontarf and Phibsboro.

Renovation questions

How much does a house renovation cost?

The price depends on the size of the house, the structural work and the level of finish. We visit your home to see the space and talk through what you want, then send a detailed price and plan for the work. Call 089 276 1391 or use the contact form to arrange a visit.

Can you remove a wall between two rooms?

Yes. We take out internal walls and fit steel beams to your engineer's specification, and the work is signed off by them.

Do you renovate older houses?

Yes. Recent work includes structural repairs to a 1908 red-brick terrace in Phibsboro, Dublin 7, where failing timber lintels and settlement along the back wall were put right.
